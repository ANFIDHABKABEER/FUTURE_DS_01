# FUTURE_DS_01 -Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business sales data to uncover revenue trends, product performance, category contribution, and regional sales distribution. The dashboard was developed in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to provide interactive business insights.

The objective is to help stakeholders understand sales performance and make data-driven decisions based on key business metrics.

---

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis

A line chart displaying sales performance over time to identify growth patterns and seasonal fluctuations.

### 2. Product Performance Analysis

A chart showing sales generated by different products, helping identify top-selling items.

### 3. Category Analysis

A category-wise breakdown of sales across:

* Furniture
* Office Supplies
* Technology

### 4. Regional Performance

A regional comparison of sales across:

* Central
* East
* South
* West

### 5. Interactive Filter

A slicer was added for Category selection, allowing users to dynamically filter dashboard results.

## Key Insights

### Category Performance

Technology and Office Supplies contribute significantly to overall sales performance.

### Regional Performance

The West region records the highest sales volume compared to other regions.

### Product Analysis

A small group of products contributes a large percentage of total revenue.

### Sales Trends

Sales show fluctuations across different periods, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

---

## Recommendations

* Increase inventory for top-performing products.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue categories.
* Expand successful strategies used in the West region.
* Investigate underperforming products and regions.
* Monitor sales trends regularly to improve forecasting.
