# FUTURE_DS_01
Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard
A dynamic, interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize retail sales data. This project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights, focusing on sales trends, product performance, regional distribution, and category breakdowns
📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard consists of four primary visual components powered by Excel PivotTables and PivotCharts:
Sales Trends Over Time (Top Left): A timeline chart tracking Sum of Sales across various order dates to identify seasonal trends and growth patterns.
Top Product Performance (Top Right): A horizontal bar chart isolating sales figures for specific high-performing items.
Category Breakdown (Bottom Left): A pie chart illustrating the market share and sales distribution among Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
Regional Sales Performance (Bottom Right): A vertical bar chart comparing total sales volume across geographic regions (Central, East, South, West).
🚀 Key Features
Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering by product category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology) that updates all charts simultaneously.
Clean Data Architecture: Structured workbook design separating raw data (Sample - Superstore), data processing/aggregations (DATA, calculations), and presentation (dashboard).
Dynamic PivotCharts: Automatically updates visual components when the underlying transactional data changes.
